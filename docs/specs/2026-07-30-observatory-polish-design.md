# Observatory Polish Design

## Intent

Refine DebtRank Globe into a more mature systemic-risk observatory while preserving the dark cinematic identity and the larger, faint, gradual atmospheric glow.

## Direction

The globe remains the hero. Controls should read as precision instrumentation floating beside it: quieter glass, clearer hierarchy, more deliberate spacing, stronger selected states, and restrained cyan/yellow/red signals. The result should feel engineered, not decorated.

## Experience

- The canvas, navigation, and control rail never overlap at supported widths.
- The fixed control group remains stable while long result rankings scroll independently.
- Dense information is easier to scan through section rhythm and tabular numerals.
- Panel and navigation motion is smooth but disabled for reduced-motion users.
- Small screens retain usable controls and unobstructed actions.

## Engineering Quality Bar

- Preserve all simulation behavior, data, and rendering interfaces.
- Avoid new dependencies and unnecessary WebGL work.
- Verify existing analysis and atmosphere tests, TypeScript, lint, and production build.
